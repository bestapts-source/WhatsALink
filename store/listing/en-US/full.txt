Message anyone on WhatsApp without saving their number.

WhatsaLink is the fastest way to start a WhatsApp chat with a phone number that is not in your contacts. Type or paste a number, tap Start Chat, and WhatsApp opens right away. No more cluttering your address book with delivery drivers, landlords, one-time buyers or sellers.

WHY PEOPLE USE WHATSALINK
• Direct chat in one tap – no contact needed
• Works with numbers from every country – use +44, +1, +91, +55, a 00 prefix, or pick a country and type the local number
• Smart paste – paste a messy email signature, ad or message and WhatsaLink finds the phone number for you
• Automatic country code – local formats like 07911 123456 become +44 7911 123456
• Recent chats – reopen a conversation or call the number in one tap, delete with undo
• Call fallback – dial the number if the person is not on WhatsApp
• Private by design – no account, no ads, no tracking. Your history stays on your device
• Lightweight and fast

PERFECT FOR
Marketplace buyers and sellers, delivery and taxi drivers, real estate agents, freelancers, small business owners, travelers and anyone who needs a quick WhatsApp chat without adding a new contact.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Type or paste a number, or any text that contains one.
2. Choose a country, or leave it on Auto.
3. Tap Start Chat. Done.
