WhatsaLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

WhatsaLink ("the app") lets you open a WhatsApp chat with a phone number without saving it as a contact. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The developer receives none of the numbers or text you enter.

Information stored on your device

  • Recent chats. The last numbers you opened (up to 10) and your small preferences, such as hidden tips, are saved locally in the app's storage on your device so that you can reopen them. They never leave your device. You can delete any entry, clear the whole list, or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Clipboard

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap the paste button. The text is placed in the input field on your screen and is processed on your device to find a phone number. It is not sent anywhere.

Phone number detection

Finding and formatting phone numbers happens entirely on your device using an offline library. No text is sent to any server or AI service.

Opening WhatsApp and calling

When you tap Start Chat, the app asks Android to open a WhatsApp link (https://wa.me/<number>). From that moment WhatsApp handles the conversation under its own terms and privacy policy. When you tap Call, the app opens your phone dialer with the number. WhatsaLink does not see or store your messages.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage or phone state.

Children

The app is a general utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
